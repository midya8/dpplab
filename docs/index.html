04 / LIKELIHOOD IS NOT INCLUSION
Two kernels.
Two meanings.
K = I - (I+L)-1. Its diagonal is item-inclusion probability. Off-diagonal entries can be negative. Pair covariance is -K[i,j]², so either sign can encode repulsion.

05 / CONDITION WITHOUT PRETENDING
Include. Exclude.
Then fix a size, if needed.
Exclude items by removing their rows and columns from L. Include A using the Schur complement L[R,R] - L[R,A] L[A,A]-1 L[A,R]. Remaining coordinates R are shown explicitly. A zero-probability event has no conditional kernel or law. No jitter, pseudoinverse or silent fallback is used.
Residual matrices condition only on include/exclude. If k is set, the selected law is additionally fixed-size and those residual inclusion-kernel minors do not describe it. k-DPPs are not ordinary DPPs in general. Exact enumeration supplies the final selected marginals and size law instead.

06 / THE CONTRACT, NOT A QUALITY CLAIM
Exact arithmetic.
Bounded ambition.
One to five items, at most 32 subsets. Reduced rational numerator magnitude and denominator are at most 10,000; input cell strings are at most 12 characters. All principal minors are checked exactly for positive semidefiniteness. Singular valid kernels are retained, not repaired. Full JSON keeps exact fractions; only bar widths use floating point.
Increasing L's scale can change expected cardinality. Conditioning on a fixed size removes common positive scale from subset probabilities. DPP repulsion is a supplied mathematical geometry, not proof of semantic diversity, fairness, relevance or downstream usefulness.
